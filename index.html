Hi, I'm Cheddar. I'm cheese that takes pictures.

Made for little hands

So simple a three-year-old can use it

One giant shutter, a few chunky buttons and no text to read. Kids point and press, or hold the shutter and say “cheese!” for a hands-free photo.

Tell Me

Photos that talk back in silly voices

Tap a photo and Cheddar says what he sees, as a Robot, Pirate, Wizard or seven others. The words appear as he speaks, so early readers can follow along.

Stories

Kids record their own storybook

Tap record and they narrate a photo in their own voice, up to 20 seconds. It replays with a slow zoom, like a tiny movie a grown-up can send to Grandma.
