Say Cheese

Take a photo by saying the magic word

Little kids can't juggle a self-timer, a tripod, and “everybody hold still.”

Long-press the giant shutter and just say “cheese!” Cheddar hears the word, counts down out loud, and snaps the shot — completely hands-free. Perfect for group photos and wobbly hands.

Tell Me

Photos that talk back — in silly voices

Kids ask a hundred questions about every picture they take.

Tap a photo and Cheddar tells you what he sees, out loud, in a voice you picked — Robot, Pirate, Wizard, and seven more. The words appear one at a time as he says them, so early readers can follow along. Tap his face for a different character. It all happens on the phone, and you can switch it off entirely.

Stories

Kids record their own storybook

Your kid tells the world's best story about a photo… then forgets it by dinner.

Tap record and let them narrate — up to 20 seconds in their own voice. It replays in a cosy “storytime mode” with a slow cinematic zoom, like a tiny movie they made themselves.

Nine games. One camera.

Tap the corner of the camera and the shelf opens. No reading, no scores, nothing to lose. Cheddar explains every game out loud.

Made for little hands

So simple a three-year-old can use it

Tiny buttons and menus full of words are a non-starter for pre-readers.

One giant shutter, a few chunky color-coded buttons, and zero text to read. Portrait-locked, one-hand friendly, and nothing to set up — kids just point and press.

Locks

Selfie LockBack camera only. Also hides the funny-face game.
Gallery LockHides the gallery button. Camera-only mode.
Activities LockHides the Activities button. Just taking pictures.
Delete ProtectionRequires solving a math problem before deleting photos and stories.

You set the limits.

Settings and sharing sit behind a quick math gate only grown-ups can pass. Cap the photos, call a break, lock the doors you don't want open.
