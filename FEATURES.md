# Cheddar — Features & Marketing Brief

*Drop this into the website repo root. It's the single source of truth for what
Cheddar does, so the site can write accurate marketing copy without access to the
app's code. Re-read it each session.*

## What Cheddar is (positioning)

Cheddar is a **native camera app made for little kids (ages 3–6)** — a safe,
playful first camera with giant buttons, zero reading required, and parents in
full control. It turns "say cheese!" into real magic: kids take photos by
*saying the word*, hear their pictures described in silly character voices, and
record their own storybook narrations. Everything stays on the device unless a
grown-up chooses to share. Built by a parent, calm by design — no ads, no
tracking, no endless-scroll tricks.

**Status:** the app is feature-complete and **launching on Google Play**
(**iOS coming later**). Everything under "In the app" below is built and working.

**Brand facts:**
- Name: **Cheddar** (store name "Cheddar Cam"). The name = the "say cheese"
  camera connection; the mascot is a friendly cheese character.
- Platform: Android now, iOS coming soon.
- Audience: kids ages 3–6 (pre-readers) + their parents (the buyers).
- Domain: cheddarcam.com.
- Look: "Soft Toybox" — warm cream background, one bright color per action
  (kids recognize buttons by color before they can read), chunky 3D "toy"
  buttons, gentle whimsical animation.
- Tagline (draft): *"The camera made for little hands."*

### Parental controls (all behind a "grown-up check" math gate)
- **Photo Limit** — cap how many photos per hour (5/10/15/20); resets each hour.
- **Session Timer** — pops a gentle "time for a break" after a set number of minutes.
- **Selfie Lock** — turn off the front camera (back camera only).
- **Gallery Lock** — hide the gallery for pure camera-only mode.
- **Delete Protection** — require solving a math problem before any photo is deleted.
- **Cheddar Says on/off** — the AI describe feature is optional; off = fully private.
- **Voices** — choose the narration character (or Surprise Me).
- **Camera Sounds** — master switch for shutter click + spoken messages; nests Silly Sounds and the spoken countdown.

## Hard constraints — what you CAN and CANNOT claim

Accuracy matters (kids' app + app-store review). Use these truthfully:

- **Photos live on the device.** They are not uploaded to any Cheddar server —
  Cheddar runs no servers. Say "photos stay on your device."
- **The only time a photo leaves the device** is (a) when a parent turns on
  **Cheddar Says**, the photo is sent to **Google's Gemini AI** to generate the
  fun description (this is optional and parent-controlled), or (b) when a
  grown-up explicitly taps **Share**. Both are opt-in / gated. Don't imply
  "100% offline" — imply "private by default, cloud only when you choose it."
- **No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no third-party tracking or analytics.**
  These are all true — lean on them.
- **Microphone** is used only for (a) hearing "cheese" during the Say Cheese
  window and (b) recording Stories. Speech is handled by the phone's built-in
  recognition. Story recordings stay on the device.
- **Anything that leaves the device or deletes a photo sits behind the grown-up
  math gate.**
- **Android only right now** — always pair Play Store call-to-action with
  "iOS coming soon." Don't show an App Store badge as if it's live.
- **Don't promise Quests as available** — it's on the roadmap, not shipped.
  Only mention it as "coming soon" if at all.
- A **privacy policy is required** and should be linked (cheddarcam.com/privacy).

## Marketing angles (ammo for copy)

**Why kids love it**
- They take photos by *saying a magic word* — "cheese!" — instead of hunting for a button.
- Their pictures *talk back*: a silly Robot or Pirate voice describes what they shot.
- They record their *own* voice telling a story, then watch it play like a little movie.
- Giant, colorful buttons and goofy sounds — it feels like a toy, not a tool.
- No reading needed, so a 3-year-old is fully independent.

**Why parents appreciate it**
- **Real limits:** photo caps, break timers, and a camera-only mode keep sessions healthy.
- **Private by default:** photos stay on the phone; the AI feature is optional and off-switchable; no ads, no tracking, no accounts.
- **Grown-up gate:** deleting, sharing, and settings all require solving a quick math problem, so little hands can't undo things.
- **Calm by design:** no streaks, no notifications, no manipulative "just one more" mechanics — it respects both the kid and the parent.
- Made by a parent, for their own kid.

**Why it's unique**
- A **voice-activated shutter** built around the universal "say cheese" — the name and the magic are the same idea.
- **AI that narrates a child's photos in character voices** — playful, not clinical.
- Kids become **storytellers**, recording their own narrations that replay cinematically.
- Deliberately **un-addictive** in a category full of dopamine traps.

**Why it's easy**
- One giant shutter dominates the screen; every action is a big color-coded button.
- Zero text roadblocks — spoken and pictured, not written.
- Portrait-locked, one-hand friendly, nothing to configure to start shooting.
