Frequently asked questions

Everything about how Cheddar works and how we keep your family's photos private.

What ages is Cheddar for?

Ages 3 and up, pre-readers especially. The buttons are giant and color-coded, with nothing to read, so little ones can use it on their own.

How much does Cheddar cost?

Cheddar is free, with no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Is Cheddar safe, and does it track my child?

It's safe, and it doesn't track your child. Cheddar can't reach the internet, has no accounts and collects no personal information, and contains no ads, analytics or tracking code. Settings and sharing sit behind a math gate that little hands can't solve. The specifics are on our privacy page.

Does Cheddar need an internet connection?

No. Cheddar works offline and never connects to anything: the Android app has no internet permission, and the iPhone app contains no networking code. Every feature runs on your device.

Where are photos stored, and how does sharing work?

Photos live inside Cheddar, in the app's private storage on your phone, not in your camera roll or a cloud library. A grown-up can save a keeper to the camera roll, or save everything at once from Settings. Sharing is always started by a grown-up, behind the math gate, through the phone's share sheet. A Tell Me or a Story can be sent as a little video.

How do I delete photos or app data?

Any photo can be deleted inside the app, along with its story and description. Turn on Delete Protection and deleting asks for the math gate first. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes every photo, story and setting, so save the keepers to your camera roll first. Photos already saved there stay on your phone.

How do the parental controls work?

Settings sit behind a math gate kids can't solve. You can cap photos per hour, set a play timer with a break, lock the selfie camera, the gallery or the games, and put deleting behind the gate too. It's also where you choose Cheddar's voice or switch Tell Me off. There are no streaks or notifications nudging kids to keep going.

What is “Tell Me”?

Tap a photo and Cheddar describes it out loud in one of ten character voices, such as Santa, Robot, Wizard or Pirate. The description and the voice are made right on your phone, and the photo is never uploaded. A grown-up can pick the voice or switch Tell Me off.

What games are there, and does Explorer recognize faces?

Nine games sit on a shelf, one tap from the camera. Each is spoken aloud, so a three-year-old can play without reading, and there are no timers or scores. In Explorer, Cheddar gives your child a mission, such as “find three comfy chairs!”, and checks each find on the phone. It knows everyday objects, colors and whether someone is smiling. It never identifies who anyone is, and nothing it notices is stored or sent. Any photo can also be decorated with stickers, frames and filters, and the original is never changed.

How does “Say Cheese” work, and what does the microphone do?

Long-press the big yellow shutter and say “cheese!” Cheddar hears the word through your phone's built-in speech recognition, counts down out loud and takes the photo. The only other thing the microphone does is record Stories, where a child narrates a photo for up to 20 seconds. Recordings are saved only on the device.

Is Cheddar on iPhone?

Cheddar is on Android now, and the iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store.
